# Contributing

Welcome! Manawa-Radar is an open-source research effort with an eventual medical-grade design objective.

1. Open an issue describing the change, intended subsystem, evidence and any safety implications.
2. Include editable source files **and** neutral manufacturing exports for CAD changes.
3. Include reproducible test instructions and identify simulated vs real measurements.
4. Do not introduce clinical claims, emergency thresholds or automated medical actions without approved evidence and risk review.
5. Do not commit patient-identifiable data, credentials or unlicensed third-party models.
6. Record unresolved decisions in QUESTIONS.md and keep unrelated work moving.

**Licences are pending formal adoption**; contact maintainers before substantial code or CAD contributions that depend on specific relicensing terms.
