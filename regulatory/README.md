# Regulatory and quality planning

The goal is a medical-grade development process, **not a present claim of medical-device approval**.

Determine intended use, market-specific classification and applicable standards with qualified regulatory specialists. Candidate frameworks include ISO 13485, ISO 14971, IEC 62304, IEC 62366-1, IEC 60601 family where applicable, IEC 81001-5-1 and relevant radio approvals.

Maintain design inputs, traceability, hazard controls, verification evidence, clinical evaluation and change control. Open-source publication alone does not establish safety or certification.
